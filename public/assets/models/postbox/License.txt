Tiny Town - postbox
===================

postbox-eiir.glb, postbox-vr.glb, postbox-evii.glb, postbox-gr.glb, postbox-eviii.glb,
postbox-gvir.glb, postbox-ciiir.glb
  Original models made for this project by scripts/build-postbox-cyphers.py (Blender); dedicated to
  the public domain (CC0 1.0): http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Each file is the project's own postbox (composed/postbox.glb, built from primitives by
  scripts/compose-models.mjs, CC0) with a royal cypher (Elizabeth II, Victoria, Edward VII,
  George V, Edward VIII, George VI, Charles III) as raised relief. The cyphers are traced from a
  cypher map supplied by the project owner; no attribution is required.

  The cypher mesh embeds no texture: every face samples one texel of the colour atlas of Kenney's
  City Kit (Roads), ../roads/Textures/colormap.png (www.kenney.nl, CC0 1.0,
  https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads). The postbox itself uses flat material colours.
