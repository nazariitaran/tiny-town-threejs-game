Tiny Town - burger restaurant
=============================

burger-restaurant.glb
  Original model made for this project by scripts/build-burger-restaurant.py (Blender); dedicated to the
  public domain (CC0 1.0): http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  The model embeds no texture: every face samples the colour atlas of Kenney's City Kit (Roads),
  ../roads/Textures/colormap.png (www.kenney.nl, CC0 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads).
