Tiny Town - parking lots
========================

parking-small.glb, parking-medium.glb, parking-large.glb
  Original models made for this project by scripts/build-parking.py (Blender); dedicated to the
  public domain (CC0 1.0): http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  They embed no texture: every face samples the colour atlas of Kenney's City Kit (Roads),
  ../roads/Textures/colormap.png (www.kenney.nl, CC0 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads).

road-joint-*.glb
  Kenney City Kit (Roads) road pieces (road-straight, road-bend-square, road-intersection-line,
  road-crossroad-line, road-end-round, road-crossing) with the centre line removed on the sides that
  join a car park, by scripts/build-parking.py. Original models by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed
  CC0 1.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
