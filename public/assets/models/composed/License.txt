Tiny Town - composed models
===========================

These GLBs are generated by scripts/compose-models.mjs.

bus-stop.glb, fence-tall.glb, fence-small.glb
  Assembled/re-centred from Kenney CC0 models (textures embedded):
    - City Kit (Commercial) 2.1  detail-overhang-wide   https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
    - Holiday Kit                bench                  https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit
    - City Kit (Roads)           road-sign-street       https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
    - City Kit (Suburban) 2.0    fence                  https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
    - Fantasy Town Kit           fence                  https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit

tulips-a.glb, tulips-b.glb, tulips-c.glb  (WP-23)
  Three Kenney Nature Kit 2.1 flowers each (flower_red/yellow/purple A, B, C), recoloured
  (metalness 0, colours converted to linear, leaves green): https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
  Original models by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0 1.0.

swimming-pool.glb, fountain.glb
  Assembled from Kenney CC0 models (textures embedded):
    - Fantasy Town Kit           fountain-square, fountain-round-detail  https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit
    - City Kit (Commercial) 2.1  detail-parasol-a, detail-parasol-b      https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
  Original models by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0 1.0:
  http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

postbox.glb
  Original primitive model made for this project; dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0).
