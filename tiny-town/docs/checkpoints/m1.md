## Playtest friction (real input, desktop 1280×720 + Pixel 7)
1. **False-negative tooltip after placement.** After a successful click, hover re-evaluates the now-occupied cell and shows a red "Something is already here" (every placement in `playtest-log.txt`).
2. **Stale refusal tooltip.** A refusal reason stays visible after the next successful placement, even after a tool switch ("Bus stops need to be next to a road" shown after placing a lamppost).
3. **Walkway visual.** A thin red-edged strip, easy to confuse with a fence (`m1-play/08-town.png`, centre-left).
4. **Lamppost and postbox too small** to read at the default zoom. The lamppost is a grey hook; the postbox is a red dot.
5. **Mobile title framing.** At 390×844 the title camera is too close, so the plot fills the screen as a flat green area (`m1/mobile-title.png`). The design brief wants a diorama on the horizon.
6. **Mobile stats row grouping.** Icons sit closer to the next number than to their own ("5 👤 14 📍"). Labels are hidden on mobile, which makes this worse (`m1/mobile-sample-town.png`).
7. **Mobile hint.** "Tap to build · two fingers move the camera" is shown when no tool is selected.
8. **Dock covers the front rows of the plot** at the default camera (cells z ≥ 19 near the centre). Acceptable with WASD/drag pan; noted for the camera default distance.
9. Not a defect: the bus stop can stay after its road is repainted. It's a sandbox and the plan's rule table doesn't cover it, so the integrator decided to allow it.

## Independent review (M1)
Reviewer average **1.6**; the integrator's was 1.85. The reviewer marked Art direction, Hero, Interactables, World and UI at 1.5 each. It flagged one standing automatic failure: on mobile, the dock plus the hint cover about 30% of the view, including placed houses. Its extra defects:
- desktop stats misgrouping
- keyboard cues on touch
- tall and low fences look identical
- off-palette lavender pavement
- houses only as wide as a road lane
- decor tufts read as birds
- hint pill covers the scene
- square isolated road tile
- generic ground ghost
- category tab styled like a selected tool
- mobile tray clipping with no scroll cue
- title block overlaps the plot corner
- sand-brown invalid tint
- no feedback when a road removes a fence
- "Paused" menu title
